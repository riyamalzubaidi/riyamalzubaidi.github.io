About Me

Howdy! I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University, working under the supervision of Prof. Nitesh Saxena at the SPIES Lab (Security and Privacy In Emerging computing and networking Systems).

My research lies at the intersection of cybersecurity and artificial intelligence. I am interested in how AI can be used to detect and defend against emerging threats, and, equally, in how AI systems themselves can be attacked, misused, or made to leak sensitive information. My goal is to build intelligent systems that are secure, trustworthy, and privacy-preserving for everyone who uses them.

Research Interests

My current and planned work focuses on the following directions:

Security of AI / LLM Systems AI for Threat Detection Adversarial Machine Learning Privacy & Data Leakage Usable Security Deepfake & Social Engineering Defense

My prior work covers:

Cryptography & Cryptanalysis Chaos-Based Encryption Swarm Intelligence & Evolutionary Algorithms Internet of Things
